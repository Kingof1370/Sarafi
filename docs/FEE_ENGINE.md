# VELYXORA EXCHANGE - FEE ENGINE
This manual details the VIP trading schedules, commission tiers, and withdrawal fees of Velyxora Exchange.

## VIP Trading Volume Tiers
- **Level 0 (Standard):** Maker 0.10%, Taker 0.20% (Volume < 100k USD)
- **Level 1 (VIP Tier 1):** Maker 0.05%, Taker 0.15% (Volume >= 100k USD)
- **Level 2 (VIP Tier 2):** Maker 0.02%, Taker 0.10% (Volume >= 1M USD)
- **Level 3 (VIP Tier 3):** Maker 0.00%, Taker 0.05% (Volume >= 10M USD)

## Referral & Affiliate Commission Split
Referred users' trading transaction fees are automatically split:
- **Affiliate share:** 20% cashback goes directly to the referrer's balance.
- **Platform revenue:** 80% remains in the exchange revenue pool.
