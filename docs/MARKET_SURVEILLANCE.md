# VELYXORA EXCHANGE - MARKET SURVEILLANCE & FRAUD DETECTION
Our surveillance core runs real-time pattern checks on executed trades and order velocity to prevent market manipulation.

## Fraud Patterns Flagged
- **Wash Trading:** Trade execution where buyer and seller accounts are identical.
- **Spoofing & Layering:** Placing large orders with immediate cancellations to create a false impression of liquidity.
- **Quote Stuffing:** Flooding the gateway with excessive orders/cancellation rates to trigger latency overheads on counterparties.
