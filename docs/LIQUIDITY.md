# VELYXORA EXCHANGE - LIQUIDITY MANAGEMENT
Tracks and analyzes order book microstructure quality.

## Microstructure Statistics
- **Spread:** Absolute price difference between the best bid and best ask.
- **Weighted Mid Price:** Midpoint weighted by depth size of both sides.
- **Depth Imbalance:** Imbalance ratio between buy and sell volumes.
- **Market Health Score:** Aggregated health metric of liquidity density.
- **Surveillance Patterns:** Detects wash trading and cancellation spam velocity abuse.
