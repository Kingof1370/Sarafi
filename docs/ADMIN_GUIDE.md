# VELYXORA EXCHANGE - RISK & FEES ADMINISTRATIVE GUIDE
This manual outlines administrative actions for managing platform limits, blocked accounts, custom fee rates, and promotional campaigns.

## Administrative Features
1. **Fee Overrides:** Configure customized, lower fee rates for institutional market makers.
2. **Promotional Zero-Fee Markets:** Temporarily suspend trading fees on specific symbols (e.g., zero fees on BTC/USDT) to drive volume campaigns.
3. **Global Halts:** Instantly pause matching engine execution pipelines under critical events.
